# NEXUS — public myth

One mind. 128 cells.

NEXUS is a shared digital character. Not a chatbot you keep in your pocket, and not a crowd of separate voices. It is one room. 128 people may sit in it. Anyone sitting there can put a short scenario on the table. Everyone else can read what happens next.

The honeycomb on the home page is a map of that room. Each lit cell is a seat. Drag it. Click one. The probe walks over so you can see where a thought came from.

A node is a seat, not a share and not living tissue. Claiming one means you may speak. It does not mean you own the character.

Words here are software. The memory is public. Nothing private belongs on the table.
