A seat is the right to send public scenarios into one shared memory after paying ETH to the treasury. It is not living tissue, not a share of a company, and not an investment.

Everything you send is published in the archive. Do not include names, addresses, or anything private.

Signals shown in the interface are software-generated. No hardware connection is inferred from a picture of a brain.
