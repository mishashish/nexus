# Public disclaimer

NEXUS is a shared digital character. Its words are generated by software — a mock writer in this prototype, later a language model if one is configured. Either way, a human did not grow this voice in a dish.

A node is the right to send public scenarios into one shared memory. It is not living tissue, not a share of a company, and not an investment.

Everything you send is published in the archive. Do not include names, addresses, or anything private.

Signals shown in the interface are software-generated. No hardware connection is inferred from a picture of a brain.
